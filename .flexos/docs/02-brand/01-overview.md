# Brand

Sophia Petrillo meets a qualified therapist.

## The look
- **Colours:** cream background `#FFF8F0`, warm ink `#3A2A20` for words, and one peach to pink gradient
  (`#E9A16B` → `#E77FA0`) for every main button and for Honey's face. Borders a soft sand `#F1E3D3`.
- **Type:** Georgia (a serif) for headings, the system font for everything else.
- **Shapes:** pill buttons, rounded fields, Honey's words in a white bubble beside her round gradient face.

## The tone
Warm, quick, honest; see Honey's voice.

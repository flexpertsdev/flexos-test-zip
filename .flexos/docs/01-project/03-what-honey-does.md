# What Honey does

- **Chat with Honey.** Talk to her any time, about an ex, work, family or just you. Warm, quick, honest.
- **Roast my profile.** Paste a dating profile and Honey roasts it. No sign-in; saving it asks for one. The hook.
- **Honey remembers.** Every ex and every breakthrough, so she picks up where you left off.

None of the three is built yet (`.flexos/apps/web/index.md`).

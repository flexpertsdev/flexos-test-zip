# Overview and vision

> In short: a sassy AI therapist-friend that pairs real psychological insight with wit and radical honesty.

## For whom
Young adults and working professionals.

## What makes it different
Honey remembers, and Honey tells you the truth.

## What it offers
- **Chat with Honey**: talk to her any time; she remembers every ex and every breakthrough.
- **Roast my profile**: paste your dating profile, Honey roasts it. No sign-in needed; it is the hook.
- Free, Pro and Premium plans (see Pricing).

## Where it stands (4 November 2026)
The website is live at https://aww-honey-test.flexos.site, but only its sign-up page is built, and that does not sign
anyone in yet. Honey herself (the chat, the roast, the memory) is planned and drawn, not built: there is no AI in
the code.

# Aww Honey, as it came

## What was in it, and where it went

  https://aww-honey-test.flexos.site.
- `flexos/`, the project's own notes from before:

  - `apps/web/` (design, 8 drawn pages, 3 features, 2 flows, data, Neon) → `.flexos/apps/web/`, each page and

  - `connections/` (Neon, Otp.dev) → `.flexos/connections/`.
- Its shortcuts (symlinks and their `↗ … .md` notes in `linked/`, `uses/` and `steps/` folders) became `related:`
  lines; nothing was copied twice.
